Midnight Fridge Roast — one line roasting a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight.

## The entry

> Sorry, the fridge is closed after midnight; the leftovers filed a complaint and I am legally required to ignore you until breakfast.

132 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–200 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **On subject** — a one-line roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight, spoken in the fridge's own voice.
2. **Real joke, surprise turn** — the leftovers filed a complaint, so the fridge is legally required to ignore you until breakfast.
3. **Tight** — one line that reads cleanly aloud, no wasted words, no second joke crowding the first.
4. **The midnight refusal is the target** — the laugh comes from the fridge's petty rule, not from generic smart-home grumbling.
5. **Sounds said aloud** — it reads like something a person would actually say at the fridge door at midnight.
